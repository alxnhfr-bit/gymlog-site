NU/PR – Lifting Log

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

NU/PR doesn’t collect your training data. Your training log stays on your iPhone. There’s no account, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer never receives your workouts. The developer only receives what you choose to send through the feedback form.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter (workouts, sets, templates, exercises, notes, bodyweight entries and settings) is stored only in the app’s own storage on your device. It isn’t uploaded anywhere.

What can leave your phone

Backups you make. “Back up now” creates a backup file and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or email. From there, the service you picked handles it under its own privacy policy. The app only reads a backup file when you pick one to restore.

Checking for app updates. When the app starts, it asks Expo’s update service (EAS Update, run by 650 Industries, Inc.) whether a newer version of the app is available. That request includes the platform (iOS), the app version, identifiers of the app update that’s installed, and a random identifier the app creates for your installation. Like any internet request, it also shows your IP address to Expo’s servers. It contains none of your training data. See Expo’s privacy policy.

Feedback you send. “Send feedback” in Settings (and the link on the support page) opens a form hosted by Tally (Tally BV, Belgium; data hosted in the EU). If you send it, the developer receives what you entered: the type of message, your description, a screenshot if you attach one and your email address if you give one. When you open the form from the app, it also includes the app version, the iOS version and your iPhone model, so a bug can be reproduced. Nothing from your training log is included unless you type it in. Feedback is used only to answer you and to fix and improve the app, and is deleted on request. See Tally’s privacy notice.

Beta testing through TestFlight. While the app is in beta, Apple’s TestFlight may collect crash reports, basic usage information and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send, and shares them with the developer. This is covered by Apple’s TestFlight terms and privacy policy.

Lock screen and Dynamic Island

During a workout, the app shows a Live Activity on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island: the workout’s name, the next set and the rest timer. Anyone who can see your lock screen can see it. The app creates and updates it on your device; it doesn’t use a push notification service, so none of it leaves your phone. You can turn Live Activities off in the iOS Settings app under NU/PR. The app sends no notifications.

What the app doesn’t do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking across apps and websites
  • No selling or sharing of your data
  • No access to Apple Health, your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay there until you delete them.

Children

The app isn’t directed at children under 13, and it doesn’t collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or to have feedback you sent deleted: use the support form or open an issue on GitHub.
