Support

Questions, problems or ideas for the app

Get in touch: send a bug report, an idea or a question. Describe what you were doing and what happened; a screenshot helps. Leave your email if you’d like an answer. In the app, the same form is in Settings › Send feedback, and it adds the app and iOS version for you.

Back up your training log

Your log is stored only on your iPhone. In the app, open Settings › Your data › Back up now and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. Do this now and then; the footer shows when you last did.

Move to a new phone

Back up on the old phone, install NU/PR on the new one, then open Settings › Your data › Restore from a backup… and pick the file. Restoring replaces what’s on the new phone, and the app keeps a safety copy first.

The rest timer on the Lock Screen

During a workout, NU/PR shows the next set and the rest countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. If you don’t see it, check the iOS Settings app › NU/PR › Live Activities.

Delete your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
